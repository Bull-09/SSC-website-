Home Renovation Checklist for Surat Homes

A good renovation sequence saves cost, reduces rework and makes contractor coordination easier.

Renovation work can include demolition, plastering, plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, flooring, false ceiling, painting and final finishing. The biggest mistakes usually happen when these trades are done in the wrong order.

Recommended renovation sequence

  1. Site inspection and scope planning
  2. Structural or leakage repairs
  3. Plumbing and electrical concealed work
  4. Plastering, waterproofing and surface preparation
  5. Flooring and false ceiling
  6. Painting and final finishing

Why one coordinated contractor helps

When multiple vendors work separately, responsibility becomes unclear. Coordinated renovation helps reduce delays, repeated breaking, mismatched levels and finishing issues.

Questions before starting

  • What work is essential and what is optional?
  • Which areas need waterproofing before finishing?
  • Where will furniture and family members shift during work?
  • What is the realistic timeline?
