Industrial Renovation in Sachin GIDC: Planning Guide

Plan factory renovation in Sachin with a clear civil scope, access arrangements and production schedule.

By Siddh Sai Corporation · Published 24 October 2026

Start with the building and the production process

List the spaces that need work: production bays, stores, dispatch routes, offices, washrooms and external drainage. Mark machinery that cannot move and routes that must stay open. A useful site brief records both building defects and operating restrictions.

Plan work zones before mobilising

Agree on material access, temporary barriers, dust control and waste removal. Coordinate electrical isolation and machinery protection with the facility team. Breaking, drilling and work at height need an agreed work method and site permissions.

Compare a complete scope

Ask whether the proposal covers removal, substrate repair, waterproofing, floor levels, service coordination and final finishing. A low initial figure can leave essential preparation outside the contract. Record quantities and assumptions before comparing quotes.

Arrange a site discussion

For a Sachin property, share the exact location, photographs, approximate floor area and shutdown windows. Siddh Sai Corporation can discuss the renovation scope and inspection next steps from its Surat office.

What to share for a site discussion

  • Exact site address and building use
  • Photographs of the affected areas and available drawings
  • Approximate scope, access restrictions and operating hours
  • Preferred timeline and any shutdown windows
