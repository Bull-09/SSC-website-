Coastal conditions and RCC repair

Being close to the sea, buildings in Hazira face salty, humid air that speeds up corrosion of steel reinforcement and roof sheets. Spalling concrete and rusted steel should be repaired properly, not just patched and painted. We assess the damage, agree the repair method and protect the repaired area.

Vendor and safety requirements

Larger plants in Hazira have contractor onboarding, permit-to-work and safety rules. Share these requirements with your enquiry so that our documentation, manpower and supervision can be planned to match.

Frequently asked questions

Do you take projects in Hazira and Ichchhapore?

Yes. Share the facility location, the scope and any contractor registration or safety requirements, and we will confirm site arrangements.

Why does RCC deteriorate faster in Hazira?

Salty, humid coastal air speeds up corrosion of reinforcement, which expands and cracks the concrete cover. The repair must deal with the corroded steel, not just the cracked surface.

Can you build heavy-duty yards and roads?

Yes. RCC road construction, bitumen roads and paver block yards are core services for us.
