False Ceiling Guide for Surat Apartments

A false ceiling can improve lighting, hide services and give rooms a premium finish when planned correctly.

False ceiling work should never be treated as only decoration. It affects room height, lighting comfort, wiring access, fan placement and the final painting finish.

What to decide before installation

  • Gypsum, POP or grid ceiling requirement
  • Room height after ceiling drop
  • Light layout and driver access
  • Fan, AC and curtain positions
  • Painting finish after board joint treatment

Lighting planning matters

Too many lights can create glare, and too few can make the room dull. Plan general lighting, task lighting and accent lighting before the framework starts.

Coordinate with electrical and painting

False ceiling work should be planned with electrical lines and final painting. This avoids cutting finished surfaces later.

For Surat homes: Check leakage above the ceiling area before closing it. Hidden dampness can damage ceiling boards later.
