Flooring Options for Surat Homes: Tiles, Marble, Granite and More

The best flooring choice depends on use, budget, maintenance, moisture and the overall renovation plan.

Flooring changes the feel of a home, but it also affects levels, doors, skirting, furniture movement and cleaning. Choose flooring with both appearance and practicality in mind.

Popular options

  • Vitrified tiles: practical, durable and widely used in apartments.
  • Marble: premium natural look, needs skilled installation and maintenance.
  • Granite: strong choice for stairs, kitchens and high-use areas.
  • Wooden flooring: warm look, best for selected interior zones.
  • Epoxy: useful for commercial and utility spaces.

Check levels before installation

Uneven levels can create door clearance issues and water flow problems. Good flooring work starts with surface preparation, layout planning and proper slope where needed.

Coordinate with renovation work

Flooring should be timed after major concealed plumbing/electrical work and before final painting touch-ups.
