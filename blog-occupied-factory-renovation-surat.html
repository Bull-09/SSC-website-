Renovating an Operating Factory in Surat: Phasing Checklist

Plan access, shutdowns and handovers when renovation happens around ongoing production.

By Siddh Sai Corporation · Published 24 October 2026

Create an operating constraints list

Record production shifts, loading times, sensitive stock and machinery access. Include routes for staff and emergency access. The renovation schedule should reflect these constraints before labour and materials arrive.

Agree isolation and separation

Use site-approved barriers and work methods for dust, noise and debris. Coordinate power and utility isolation with authorised site personnel. Workers need a clear boundary between production activity and construction zones.

Build in inspection windows

Do not plan handover solely around the last coat of paint. Preparation, repair checks, curing and cleaning can determine when an area is ready. Agree release criteria with the facility team before starting each phase.

Document changes during work

Keep a record of newly discovered defects, revised quantities and approved variations. A short regular site review helps align operations and contractors. For a Surat factory, share shutdown availability with the first enquiry.

What to share for a site discussion

  • Exact site address and building use
  • Photographs of the affected areas and available drawings
  • Approximate scope, access restrictions and operating hours
  • Preferred timeline and any shutdown windows
