Plumbing and Electrical Planning During Home Renovation

Hidden lines decide how reliable your finished home feels after renovation.

Plumbing and electrical work should be planned before plaster, false ceiling, flooring and painting. If these lines are changed later, finished surfaces may need to be broken again.

Plan these before finishing

  • Bathroom and kitchen water points
  • Drainage slope and pipe access
  • Switch boards and appliance load
  • AC, geyser, washing machine and kitchen equipment points
  • Concealed wiring routes and inspection access

Why coordination matters

A false ceiling may hide wiring, but it should still allow access where required. Flooring levels may affect bathroom drainage. Painting should happen after concealed work is tested.

Reduce future repair cost

Good MEP planning reduces leakage, short circuits, overloading and unnecessary wall breaking later.
