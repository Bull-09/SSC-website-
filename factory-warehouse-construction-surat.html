What we construct

  • Factory sheds for textile, engineering and processing units.
  • Warehouses and godowns with floors built for racks, trolleys and forklifts.
  • RCC industrial buildings for multi-floor units and offices.
  • Site works: RCC and bitumen roads, paver block yards, drainage, compound walls and gates.
  • Services and finishing: plastering, flooring, electrical, plumbing, false ceiling and painting.
  • Solar: ground-mounted and rooftop solar with civil foundations.

How a construction project runs

We work to the drawings and specifications from the owner's architect and structural engineer. Before starting, we agree the scope, line-item quantities, milestones and inspection points. During the project you get regular progress updates, and at handover a written completion record.

Why industrial clients choose us

Siddh Sai Corporation was founded by Mr. Sanjay Shinkar, a civil engineering diploma holder, over 30 years ago. Factories, warehouses, roads, waterproofing and electrical works all come under one contractor, so there is one point of accountability from foundation to handover.

Areas covered

We take enquiries from Surat, Sachin GIDC, Surat SEZ, Pandesara, Kadodara, Palsana and Hazira, as well as the rest of Surat. Our office is in Piplod, Surat.

Frequently asked questions

Have you built factories in Sachin GIDC and Surat SEZ?

Yes. We built power loom factories Nos. 30 to 45 at PTO Sachin GIDC and the Zota Healthcare factory and warehouse in Surat SEZ.

Do you provide design and approvals?

We build to the drawings from your architect and structural engineer and coordinate with them during construction. Approvals stay with the owner's design team.

Can you also build the roads and yards inside the plot?

Yes. RCC roads, bitumen roads, paver block yards and drainage are core services for us.
