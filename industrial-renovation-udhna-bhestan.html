Industrial Renovation & Rehabilitation in Udhna and Bhestan

Udhna and Bhestan have some of Surat's older industrial and godown buildings, many of them RCC structures now 25 to 40 years old. Renovation here often starts with rehabilitation: making the structure sound before new floors, finishes or services go in.

What older Udhna and Bhestan buildings usually need

  • RCC repair: spalled concrete, exposed or rusted reinforcement in slabs, beams and columns.
  • Slab and terrace leakage: waterproofing over old slabs, often with added load from later floors.
  • Floor replacement in godowns and workshops where old floors have cracked and settled.
  • Plumbing, drainage and electrical renewal as part of the same renovation.
  • Change of use: converting an old workshop or godown into an office, showroom or a different process.

Assessment before renovation

For buildings of this age we recommend a condition assessment before agreeing finishing work. Where the damage is structural, the repair method should be confirmed by a structural engineer. We then carry out the repair, followed by renovation, as one coordinated scope.

Working in dense, busy streets

Access in Udhna and Bhestan is often tight. We plan material deliveries, debris removal and working hours around the street and the neighbouring units.

Areas covered

We take enquiries from Udhna, Udhna Udyog Nagar, Bhestan and Sachin Road, as well as the rest of Surat. Our office is in Piplod, Surat.

Frequently asked questions

My building is over 30 years old. Should I renovate or rebuild?

It depends on the structural condition. A condition assessment shows whether rehabilitation is enough or whether reconstruction makes more sense. See our guide on renovation vs rehabilitation vs reconstruction.

Do you repair rusted reinforcement and spalled concrete?

Yes. Structural repair and RCC rehabilitation is one of our core services, carried out to a method confirmed after assessment.

Can you renovate a godown into an office or showroom?

Yes. We handle the civil, flooring, false ceiling, plumbing, electrical and finishing work as one contract.
