Typical renovation scope for Sachin units

Most Sachin GIDC premises are loom sheds, processing units and warehouses that have run for years with little downtime. The renovation requests we discuss most often are:

  • Shed floors: cracked or sunken floors under loom lines and trolley routes, re-levelled and re-laid in phases.
  • Roof sheets and gutters: leaking sheet roofs, failed gutter joints and rusted purlins before the monsoon.
  • Drainage and plinth protection: waterlogging around sheds, broken storm drains and damaged plinths.
  • Internal roads and yards: RCC roads, paver block yards and loading areas for trucks and tempos.
  • Offices, washrooms and canteens: plastering, flooring, false ceiling, plumbing and electrical upgrades.
  • Fabrication: mezzanines, platforms, shed extensions and gates.

Working around running looms

Power loom and processing units rarely stop fully. We plan work bay by bay, protect machinery from dust and water, agree material routes away from production, and schedule noisy or wet work for weekly offs or holiday shutdowns where the owner can arrange them.

New sheds and extensions in Sachin

For new factory sheds, warehouse blocks or extensions on an existing plot, we handle the civil package: foundations, RCC structure, plinth, flooring, drainage, roads and finishing. Approvals and structural design stay with the owner's appointed architect and structural engineer, and we build to their drawings.

Frequently asked questions

Have you worked in Sachin GIDC before?

Yes. We constructed power loom factories Nos. 30 to 45 at PTO Sachin GIDC, delivered other industrial construction projects in the estate, and maintain printing units in Sachin.

Can you repair a loom shed floor without stopping the whole factory?

Usually the floor is repaired in phases, one bay or loom line at a time, so the rest of the unit keeps running. The exact phasing depends on the machinery layout and curing time needed.

Do you also work in Surat SEZ?
