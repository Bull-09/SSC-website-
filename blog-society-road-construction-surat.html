RCC, Bitumen or Paver Blocks for Society Roads in Surat?

Internal roads and parking surfaces should be selected based on traffic, drainage, maintenance and budget.

Housing societies in Surat often need to repair or rebuild internal roads after years of settlement, waterlogging or surface wear. The right choice depends on use.

RCC roads

RCC is strong and suitable for heavy use, long-term durability and areas where rigid pavement is preferred. Good sub-base and joints matter.

Bitumen roads

Bitumen gives a smooth road surface and is often used for access roads and larger driveway areas. Drainage and base preparation decide life.

Paver blocks

Pavers are useful for parking, pathways, society premises and areas where future access to underground services may be needed.

Do not ignore drainage

Waterlogging damages roads faster. Slope, outlets and base compaction are as important as the visible surface.
