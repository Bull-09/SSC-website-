Site visits from Surat

Our office is in Piplod, Surat, and Kadodara and Palsana are a regular drive for our team. Share the plot location on the highway or the estate name, and we will confirm a visit date.

Planning a newer unit's first renovation

Many units here are 5 to 15 years old and are now facing their first major repairs: settling floors, leaking roof joints and yards that were never built for today's truck loads. A single inspection covering all of these helps you plan the work in one shutdown instead of several.

Areas covered
